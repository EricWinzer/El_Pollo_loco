El Pollo loco

Aktionkeys:

  • A or Left Arrow - Move Left
  • D or Right Arrow - Move Right
  • Space or Up Arrow - Jump
  • S - throw bottle

Playnotes:

  • Kill adult chickens by jumping on them or throw bottles at them
  • Kill the biddies only by throwing bottles at them
  • Kill the Endboss by throwing bottles at him
  • Collect coins and bottles to increase your score and ammo
  • Avoid getting hit by chickens, biddies and the Endboss
